## 关于我们 | About Us

**ElexvxAI创新产业研发中心**（英文名：**ElexvxAI Lab**）由**宏翔商道（南京）科技发展有限公司**于 **2026年03月06日**正式成立。

**ElexvxAI Innovation Industry R&D Center**, with the English name **ElexvxAI Lab**, was officially established by **Hongxiang Shangdao (Nanjing) Technology Development Co., Ltd.** on **March 6, 2026**.

中心聚焦人工智能技术创新、产业场景应用研发、数字化转型支撑与成果转化，致力于打造集**技术研究、产品研发、系统集成、场景落地、协同创新**于一体的综合性创新研发平台。

The center focuses on AI technology innovation, industry-oriented application R&D, digital transformation enablement, and innovation commercialization. It is committed to building a comprehensive innovation platform integrating **technology research, product development, system integration, scenario implementation, and collaborative innovation**.

## 我们在做什么 | What We Do

### 人工智能技术研究 | AI Technology Research
围绕人工智能模型能力、数据分析、智能决策、自动化流程及应用算法开展研究与工程实践。  
We conduct research and engineering practice in AI model capabilities, data analytics, intelligent decision-making, workflow automation, and applied algorithms.

### 产业场景应用研发 | Industry Scenario Applications
面向企业服务、产业升级、智慧运营与数字化转型场景，研发具备实用性与可扩展性的智能解决方案。  
We develop practical and scalable intelligent solutions for enterprise services, industrial upgrading, smart operations, and digital transformation scenarios.

### 智能产品与平台建设 | Intelligent Products and Platforms
推进人工智能相关产品、工具、平台及数字系统的研发与孵化，提升技术成果产品化与工程化能力。  
We promote the development and incubation of AI-related products, tools, platforms, and digital systems, enhancing productization and engineering implementation.

### 技术融合与创新转化 | Technology Integration and Innovation Transformation
推动人工智能与软件系统、工业场景、数据平台、智能监测及相关业务体系深度融合，加快成果转化与应用落地。  
We drive the deep integration of AI with software systems, industrial scenarios, data platforms, intelligent monitoring, and related business systems, accelerating innovation transformation and real-world deployment.
